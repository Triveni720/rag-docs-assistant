# Salesforce CRM

Salesforce is a cloud customer relationship management (CRM) platform used to manage leads, contacts, accounts and opportunities.

## Standard objects

A Lead is a potential customer who has not yet been qualified. When a lead is qualified it is converted into an Account, a Contact and optionally an Opportunity. An Opportunity tracks a potential sale and its stage in the sales pipeline.

## Reports and dashboards

Salesforce reports filter and group records, for example all contacts with a birthday next month. Dashboards show charts built from reports so teams can track key metrics.

## Integration

External systems connect to Salesforce through its REST and SOAP APIs. Integration platforms such as MuleSoft are often used to sync Salesforce data with other systems like Google Calendar or an ERP.

## Agentforce

Agentforce is Salesforce's platform for building AI agents that can answer questions and take actions on CRM data.
